Support

Rocket Launch Tracker shows upcoming and past rocket launches, lets you save favorites and local reminders, and lists meteor showers, moon phases, asteroid close approaches, and NASA’s Astronomy Picture of the Day.

The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NASA or any launch provider.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub. There is no in-app account and no separate support inbox.

Permissions

  • Notifications are optional. They are local reminders for a launch you pick. The app does not send remote push notifications.
  • Calendar is optional. Add to Calendar opens the system event sheet. iOS 17 and later does not ask for calendar access first. Earlier versions ask only when you tap the button. The app does not read your calendar or your reminders.
  • The app does not ask for your location.

If launch lists are empty or out of date

Launch schedules come from Launch Library 2. The free production service allows a small number of requests per hour. The app keeps a cache and shows that cache when the service is busy or you are offline. Pull to refresh, or use Settings → Clear Cache and try again. A cleared cache needs a network connection to fill back in.

Asteroid data and the picture gallery come from NASA. Without a developer-configured NASA API key, those requests share NASA’s demo key and can be throttled.
